# Visual and owner-rules review, round 1 (commit 1522ed6, boards as of f512434)

## Verdict

FAIL on owner silkscreen rules and on schematic visual quality. 0 BLOCKER, 12 MAJOR, 7 MINOR, 4 NIT.
The Core pad labels cannot be read (they overlap each other, sit on pads and are clipped by the edge), 32 user solder pads have no label, and the Base 'LED' polygons are corrupted. Neither board carries a revision or date, and the Core has no name.
The root, fc_power and fc_rp2350a sheets have stacked symbols and overlapping text. DRC still reports 41 silk errors (Core 14, Base 31), and the project files set silk_overlap and missing_courtyard to "ignore".

Method: I ran `kicad-cli pcb render` (basic, 1600x1200 and 4000x3000) on both sides and looked at the PNGs. I also ran commons `silk_check.py` (Core 88 hits, Base 297) and `sch_visual_check.py`, which reports 13723 hits; I counted only what the PDF plot shows. Other checks: `kicad-cli pcb drc --severity-all` on a scratch copy, pcbnew queries and the schematic PDF at 300 dpi. Scripts and images are in `scratchpad/critique/visual/`.
