# Power and thermal critique (round 1): OpenAIO at 1522ed6 (board files as of f512434)

**Verdict**
- No BLOCKER. The power stage is sound: each of the 12 legs has its own 0805 bypass cap 0.27-0.39 mm from the stack, with 17-19 phase vias. The shunt path is short (/CSA+ 0.134 mOhm) and the INA186 sense lines are truly Kelvin (-IN pick-up is 0 mOhm/A for all 12 drains).
- 3 MAJOR: both buck FB nodes are routed against LMR51430 §9.4.1.2; the +5V rail runs on 0.25-0.30 mm inner tracks with vias at 2.2 A; 2S is still outside the NSG2065Q recommended VCC. 9 MINOR, 1 NIT.
- The LAYOUT_REVIEW +BATT target (0.387 mOhm mean) is not met: the board measures 0.581. GND is close to its target (0.359 vs 0.325). The thermal model gives about **20 A per motor continuous** (all 4 motors, 120 C board, good airflow). 30 A is burst only, about 10 s.

Method: the `lr_dc.py` solver (h 0.05 mm, refilled zones, 20 um via plating, stackup from the board: F/B 70 um, In 35 um, In3-In4 0.23 mm) on fresh dumps of both boards. Scripts and outputs are in `critique/power/` (`pw.py`, `kel.py`, `checks.py`, `fb.py`, `whatif.py`, `jmap.py`). R means 1 A alone, source to sink. Coordinates are Base coordinates.
