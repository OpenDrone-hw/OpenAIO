# EMC / SI critique, round 1 (perspective: emc), boards at f512434 (repo 1522ed6)

## Verdict

1. **Core: clean.** In1, In4 and B.Cu are solid single-piece GND, and B.Cu has no signals. No victim (USB, SD, IMU, crystal, ADC, video, DShot) has an aggressor within 1 mm. The crystal sits in F.Cu GND over 96 % solid In1, and the IMU has three GND planes between it and the Base. The LGA claim holds: 20/20 signals and 5/5 power pads have an orthogonal GND pad. The one Core gap is the RP2350 LX cut-out (E11).
2. **Base: not yet acceptable.** There are 5 MAJOR, 7 MINOR and 1 NIT. The MAJOR ones:
   - the u.FL RF via goes through the +BATT planes;
   - the WiFi antenna keepout lets tracks and vias in, and the re-route added some;
   - the USB pair is not a pair, and it passes a 1.1 MHz switch node at 0.16 mm;
   - netclasses are missing or do not match;
   - SD SPI0 references +BATT and runs broadside to a phase track.
3. **Method.** I refilled both boards in a directory with the .kicad_pro and .kicad_dru (`emc/run/`). The scripts are adapted from lr_si, lr_xboard and lr_aggmap (`emc/*.py`, results in `emc/emc_si.txt`). I used the actual Base stackup, including In3–In4 = 0.23 mm (lr_common said 0.3).

## Already known: confirmed still holding

- **In4/In5 nets:** 19 signal nets still have tracks on In4/In5 (+BATT). Six of them are sensitive; see E5.
- **U3 buck HF cap:** still not placed. The nearest +BATT–GND cap to the U3 VIN pins is C18 4.7 µF at 1.64 mm (U4 has C107 100 nF at 0.55 mm). This adds to the U3 switching noise next to USB1 (E3).
- **JP1.1, FL1.2, U12.7/8 edge defects:** positions are unchanged from d81e559. U12 over AE2 is part of E2.
- **Core finish-pass routes:** still present, for example I2C0.SDA at 29.1 mm.
- **FET stacking:** not re-assessed; it is outside the EMC scope.
