# OpenAIO critique round 1: DFM and assembly (perspective: dfm)

Commit 1522ed6 (board files identical to f512434: `git diff --stat f512434 1522ed6 -- hardware` is empty). Both boards were refilled in a private copy with their own .kicad_pro and .kicad_dru. Scripts and outputs are in `scratchpad/critique/dfm/`: `dump.py`, `gaps.py`, `wrl.py` (exact VRML face projection), `paste.py`, `ep.py`, `tomb.py`, `lga.py`, `fid.py`, `edge.py`, `outside.py`, `under.py`, plus `out/drc_*.json`.

## Verdict

1. **Not orderable as is: 1 BLOCKER.** The TF socket Card1 stands under the Core's lower-left corner, so the Core cannot sit flat on J90.
2. **9 MAJOR problems block a clean NextPCB turnkey order**: FET land does not match the BOM MOSFET, fine-pitch stencil apertures too small, starved LGA joint, +BATT 0.16 mm from LGA signal pads, systematic 0201 tombstone risk, Core fiducials off-board, Core missing from the Base BOM/CPL, R34 under Card1, no legal tab sites for panelization.
3. **What passes:** fab rules (track/space, drill, ring, hole-to-hole, edge, VII fill+cap in board setup), BOM MPN coverage and second-side masses all pass apart from the known items.

Counts: BLOCKER 1, MAJOR 9, MINOR 12, NIT 3.

## Already known (confirmed still holding)

- **FET stacking:** 12 high-side FETs on F and 12 low-side FETs on B, mirrored, so the exposed pads overlap for X-ray. Unchanged.
- **Original edge defects:** DRC `copper_edge_clearance` 4: JP1.1 0.000, U12.7/.8 0.000, FL1.2 0.010. Core D1.2 is no longer flagged.
- **Dangling vias:** Core `via_dangling` 28, Base 1.
- **Base vias:** Base vias are 1296 x 0.35/0.20, 42 x 0.50/0.30 and 2 x 0.45/0.25 (ring 0.075, owner-confirmed). Core vias are 251 x 0.35/0.15.
- **Base clearance errors:** now 16, down from 17. All are inner-layer gate tracks and U24.11 at 0.0898-0.090 against the 0.10 rule.
- **Rsense2:** still has no Manufacturer. It is the only placed part missing one; the 67 Core and 226 other Base BOM parts all have Manufacturer and MPN.
- **Second-side masses:** checked, no finding. Base B is the first reflow side, so its parts hang during the F reflow. The worst is DSK24 at 0.015 g on 2.18 mm² = 6.9 mg/mm² (datasheet weight). Estimates for the others: Q 6.06 mm², Rsense2 8.09 mm², U22 9.75 mm². All are far below 46 mg/mm².
- **Fab rules:** track/space checked against the NextPCB tiers on a probe DRU, no new finding. Base outer is ≥0.14 (2 oz extreme tier) and inner ≥0.10 apart from the known 0.09 items. Hole-to-hole (0.30 different net), hole clearance and edge clearance are clean apart from the known items.
